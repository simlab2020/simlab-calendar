Display Settings

Display Settings

Changes save to the database and reach the wall screen within a second — no need to touch the display. If something looks wrong on the screen, change it back here.

Board

Layout

One week fills the screen and rotates, like a daily board. Wall of weeks shows several weeks side by side, the way the paper grids are pinned up now — good for planning, harder to read from a distance.

Booking colors

Rename or recolor these and every booking already using that name updates on the board. Deleting a name leaves those bookings grey until you reassign them.

Graphics & branding

Upload image files to the GitHub repo, then put the file name here — utep-logo.png is enough if it sits beside display.html. A full https:// address works too. PNG with a transparent background looks best on a dark board. Use official UTEP artwork from University Communications and follow the campus brand guidelines.

Holiday artwork — the turkey, a Christmas graphic — goes on a day banner instead: open a day on the board and set its decoration.

Notes area

A shared scratch space under the calendar. Anyone signed in can add, edit, pin or delete notes, and they appear on every screen at once.

Equipment inventory

What the lab owns and how many. Once an item is listed here, a booking can reserve it, and the board will warn when more are booked than exist.

Blank the name to delete an item.

Board theme

Rooms

Uncheck a room to drop its column from the board without deleting any history — useful during the reconfiguration. Capacity drives the over-capacity flag in popups.

Stations

The physical positions in this room — beds, tables, chairs. Once a room has stations, a booking can say which skill happens at each one and what it needs.
