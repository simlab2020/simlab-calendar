Display Settings

Changes save to the database and reach the wall screen within a second — no need to touch the display. If something looks wrong on the screen, change it back here.

Board

Layout

One week fills the screen and rotates, like a daily board. Wall of weeks shows several weeks side by side, the way the paper grids are pinned up now — good for planning, harder to read from a distance.

Booking colors

Rename or recolor these and every booking already using that name updates on the board. Deleting a name leaves those bookings grey until you reassign them.

Notes area

A shared scratch space under the calendar. Anyone signed in can add, edit, pin or delete notes, and they appear on every screen at once.

Board theme

Rooms

Uncheck a room to drop its column from the board without deleting any history — useful during the reconfiguration. Capacity drives the over-capacity flag in popups.
