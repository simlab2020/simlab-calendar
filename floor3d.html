Welcome to the lab viewer

  • 2D Map — straight-down view, like the blueprint. Drag or use the arrow keys to move around, pinch/scroll or W/S to zoom.
  • 3D View — an angled view of the lab. Drag or A/D to turn it, arrow keys to move, pinch/scroll or W/S to zoom.
  • Walk — move through the hallways and rooms yourself. Drag to look, use the arrows (or W/A/S/D) to move.
  • Tap any room to see just what's inside it and which groups are there.
  • The edit panel that pops up can be dragged by its title bar (double-tap the bar to put it back) and made see-through with the ◐ button. It also fades out while you drag an item.
  • Groups and rotations — turn on Customize. Groups can be in any room and bed at any time: create them under Groups, pick each group's room and bed for a time slot, or use Create a rotation to build the whole schedule from a list of rooms or beds.
  • Customize turns on editing. Tap any bed, piece of equipment, or label to select it, then drag it — or use the toolbar that pops up to move, resize, reshape, raise/lower and rotate it. Labels also get corner squares (resize) and a round handle (rotate).
  • To move a door, turn on Customize and drag the blue ◀ Door ▶ marker along the wall — or onto a different wall. Tap it to change its width, flip its swing, or remove it. Rooms can have more than one door.
  • To fix walls, turn on Customize and tap any wall to remove it (use “Put back removed walls” in the side panel to restore them). Tap + Add wall to add a new one — drag it into place and drag the blue dots at its ends to stretch or angle it.
  • To put a label anywhere (a hallway, a station, a zone), turn on Customize and tap + Add label. Drag it into place, drag the corner squares to resize it, and drag the round handle to rotate it.
  • To move a room's number label (for example when 102 sits over the room next to it), turn on Customize and drag the number, or tap it and use the arrows. Reset puts it back in the middle of its room.
  • To delete a room, turn on Customize and tap its room number, then Delete room. Undo delete in the side panel brings it back. + Add room adds a new one you can size by dragging its walls.
  • To reuse a room's setup, open it in Customize, tap Copy this room's layout, then open another room and tap Paste. The layout stretches or shrinks to fit the new room.
